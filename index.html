Dr. Ankush Kisanrao Aglawe is a distinguished politician, social worker, and agricultural champion from Maharashtra. As Jila Adhyaksh, he has spent over a decade building bridges between government policy and grassroots reality.

His work spans the full spectrum of public service — from securing irrigation projects for drought-hit farmers to establishing educational institutions in underserved rural areas.

Born into a farming family, Dr. Aglawe understands the heartbeat of rural Maharashtra. His academic credentials and political acumen are matched only by his dedication to the communities he serves.
